# Nested station river comparison decision

The supplemental four-procedure study is complete. On 140 distinct stations,
current, simple-upstream, structure-upstream and matched non-upstream MAE are
1.723002, 1.725797, 1.723002 and 1.723002 mg/L. Simple aggregation is slightly
worse (−0.162% MAE reduction, 95% station interval −0.458% to +0.013%).
All structural and non-ancestor readouts were shrunk to zero by inner station
CV. This establishes no positive structural correction to the current model.
It does not establish physical equivalence or absence of river influence.

All nine packages and 27 outer station blocks are complete and replayed.
Availability matching gives 50 supported stations in at least one partition.
Receiving stations never enter donor banks or their own correction fits.
The frozen base was historically selected on this validation population;
therefore these are retrospective development results, not independent
geographical or external confirmation.

Keep the current complete predictor. Full interpretation, geographical
comparison, form-specific observation coverage and the next process question
are in the companion `doc_river_frozen_comparison_v1/research_decision.md`.
