# Co-sampled river forms and real-path geometry

Date: 2026-10-08. Earlier river-form, pathway and observed-coordination results
have been seen. This is an exploratory extension of observation availability,
not a replacement for the preceding 32-network monthly analysis.

## Scientific question

Can the accepted activity archive supply additional same-calendar-day upstream
and receiving DOC records in elongated and broad river forms? Do those records
support comparison of source-path separation and shared-corridor length against
receiving fluctuations, while retaining the original whole-network classes?

## Population and source selection

Retain the 297 mapped station networks, their 295 receiving COMIDs, original form
classes, existing mapping-quality exclusions and metadata-selected receiver
aliases. Only the established union of source-training DOC cells is accessible.
No target-role labels, new water samples or fitted model predictions are added.

Audit all accepted activities in permitted station-months. Results belonging to
one activity are averaged using the existing QC policy; the earliest known UTC
activity per station/local calendar day is primary, with all-activity daily means
kept separately. Missing clocks remain missing. Reconcile native monthly means
to the saved source-role dataset.

For each receiver, upstream candidate gauges must share at least one sampling
date with the receiver and pass the existing mapping check. Collapse gauge COMID
aliases using common-date count, then station ID. Build the nearest-downstream
disjoint frontier on the original mapped routes. Source sets are selected by
represented area, then common-date count and station ID: the largest-area pair
with at least 12 exact common dates in at least three year-months, followed by
area-ordered additions preserving those counts. Retain failed networks and all
candidate metadata. Official source-area sums must remain compatible with the
receiver area. DOC magnitudes do not select gauges, sets or sampling dates.

This differs explicitly from the prior 24-month / three-year / six-calendar-month
selection. Report old/new membership and same-day inventory separately. It does
not add independent rivers merely by adding dates to an old receiver.

## Signals and geometry

The main seasonal signal uses only year-months with at least three common dates;
subtract each station's within-month mean on the identical date panel. Require
at least 12 retained dates in three year-months. Calendar-season/year projection
and unadjusted centering on all common dates are sensitivity analyses, not a
substitute chosen by result. Save all eligibility counts.

Reuse source coordination, independent receiving SD reference and upper-quartile
excursion definitions from the preceding study. A receiving excursion contrast
needs at least five coincident and five solo-source dates. Q90 is supplementary.
Keep fixed catchment-area shares and distinguish them from measured flow weights.

Map actual cropped source-to-receiver paths, entry points on the geometric
mainstem, their distance variation and the shared suffix. Retain mapped lake and
reservoir context. Do not calibrate travel velocity, lag or dispersion to DOC.
Record clock spans, upstream-after-receiver counts and sampling gaps to show
which temporal scales the observations actually resolve.

## Comparisons and evidence

- Keep receiver-equal source coordination, relative receiving fluctuation and
  excursion differences; resample whole overlapping-catchment systems 5,000 times.
- Compare original broad versus elongated forms and the fixed >=80% represented
  area subset. Keep class counts, absent combinations and inconclusive results.
- Relate path CV and shared-corridor fraction to independently measured receiving
  signals. These are descriptive field relationships, not estimated mediation.
- Revisit all existing same-HUC4, comparable-area form pairs. Count newly
  observable pairs without selecting them by receiving DOC or effect direction.
- Choose any displayed observed waveform by sampling density, then station ID,
  year-month ID; show both forms when available. Do not choose a striking peak.

Save an inventory, co-sampled activities, geometry, analyses, bilingual figures,
reproduction commands and an English research decision. Verify source exclusion,
metadata-only selection, disjoint geometry, excursion arithmetic and full replay.
