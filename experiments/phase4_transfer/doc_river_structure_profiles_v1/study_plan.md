# River-form mechanism profiles

Date: 2026-10-07. Exploratory synthesis after the earlier results were seen.

## Question

How do the three fixed real river forms organize arrivals, shared downstream
paths and mapped storage, and which parts of that mechanism are supported by
the existing DOC observations?

## Analysis

1. Retain the 297 source-role station instances and their original outline
   classes (102 elongated, 30 mainstem dominated, 165 broad). Do not recluster
   them using DOC. Shared/nested receiving networks remain dependent geometry.
2. Join all-source area-weighted path dispersion and storage exposure from the
   complete-network experiment. Use the original station panel's HUC4 labels,
   preserving leading zeros. Other studies' geographic display metadata do not
   replace these labels.
3. For the 295 previously selected real major confluences, map LakePond and
   Reservoir reaches onto the two representative independent paths and their
   shared trunk. Measure channel length within waterbodies separately on each
   segment. Area shares are flow proxies, not measured discharge. The two
   representatives and their tributary-area shares are fixed from geometry.
4. Describe where mapped storage occurs: absent from these selected paths,
   independent paths only, shared trunk only, or both. Absence on a selected
   corridor does not imply absence in the entire network. A waterbody crossing
   the junction can occupy both segments; segment allocations do not count it
   as two independent physical waterbodies.
5. Summarize continuous descriptors within each original form. Report 5,000
   HUC4 block-bootstrap intervals for station-equal means, observed medians
   and distributions. Use the same 22 environment-matched elongated/broad
   pairs for contrasts; do not rematch or exclude pairs according to DOC.
6. Reuse the mean-delay-one identical-input routing experiment on these same
   networks to describe peak redistribution. Keep it separate from actual
   monthly DOC CV, seasonal amplitude and concentration level. Recompute the
   original matched DOC contrasts without fitting a new explanatory model.
7. Retain the 59 monitored corridors / 22 receivers / 11 systems as a distinct
   observational scale. Reuse their measured outlet/mixture variability and
   continuous-geometry results; do not substitute whole-network covariates for
   their monitored corridors or turn simulations into observed effect sizes.

## Outputs and interpretation

A station-level integrated profile, mapped storage partition inventory,
continuous class summaries, fixed matched contrasts, original representative
maps, bilingual figures and an English research decision. No new neural
training, physical travel-time fitting, field event-peak estimation or outcome
selected morphology groups. The sampling audit defines what the field data can
resolve. This step produces a coherent river-structure evidence chain rather
than another unconstrained survey of terrestrial source conditions.
