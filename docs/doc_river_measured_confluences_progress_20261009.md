# Measured confluence research progress

The next field step is complete in
`experiments/phase4_transfer/doc_river_measured_confluences_v1/`.

Five Tom's Creek confluences, two baseflow campaigns and thirty downstream
position means now connect incoming DOC/discharge to receiving DOC, local
channel dimensions and tracer-derived residence time. All positions and both
seasonal directions are retained. The 552 geometry rows contain 69 distinct
width transects; widths are weighted by transect rather than repeated depth
positions.

Similar downstream widening can accompany opposite DOC departures. The
stronger descriptive field lead is relative hydraulic residence time, which
has a negative rank association with two-campaign DOC departure across the
five confluences. Seasonal reversals at two configurations show that fixed
geometry alone does not specify the response sign. The research decision
records all ten comparisons, source-field discrepancies and geometry weights.

This acquired archive provides laboratory DOC and tracer hydrology, but no
synchronized storm branch–branch–outlet curves or complete rooted whole-network
geometry. The published Turbolo pair was revisited only as source context; its
previous hourly-response and optical-quality findings were not repackaged as
new evidence. The next dynamic test needs paired incoming waves and receiver
records, then an arrival/residence operator conditional on those measured inputs.

Analysis is reproducible with the experiment's fetch, analyze, verify and plot
scripts. Independent scalar checks cover all thirty observations, ten campaign
means and ten geometry reaches. No training, tuning or automation is started.
