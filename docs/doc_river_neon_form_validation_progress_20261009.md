# Whole river-network form: independent laboratory-DOC comparison completed

Experiment: `experiments/phase4_transfer/doc_river_neon_form_validation_v1`.
No model training or recurring automation was started.

## Work completed

- Acquired public registered S2/buoy coordinates and historical alternatives for
  the 27 river/stream sites in the NEON-authored laboratory-DOC archive.
- Measured 23 complete upstream network geometries; retained 13 under the
  unchanged geometry/location criteria. Preserved all exclusions.
- Combined nested networks into 11 holdout/resampling groups; identified three
  sites sharing channels with ST357, leaving 10 sites/eight groups without that
  overlap.
- Applied the original geometry-only class centers; no new DOC-driven clustering.
- Evaluated fixed context/footprint/branching/path comparisons for season-balanced
  DOC level and monthly relative IQR, with 5,000 whole-group bootstrap draws.
- Completed common-calendar, non-overlapping-network and domain-holdout checks,
  actual network maps, independent score verification and scientific closeout.

## Findings

The full new cohort does not reproduce a general structural improvement:
branching gain for DOC level is −2.08% (95% interval −11.56% to +6.59%).
The predeclared non-ST357-overlapping subset gives +13.32% (+1.48% to +32.41%),
six/eight groups positive, while the common-window gain is +3.18% with an interval
crossing zero. Branching information is conditional, not uniformly transferable.

No robust DOC variability benefit was established. No elongated/broad pairs
met the declared comparable-environment criteria, so a direct shape ordering
cannot be estimated. The scientific question remains about river structure;
land cover and climate are context controls, not the research outcome.

## Closeout

This bounded comparison is complete. The report keeps favorable and unfavorable
populations together. Stronger inference requires additional comparable real
networks and original sample QC; transport attribution additionally requires
simultaneous incoming-branch/outlet DOC and discharge. Do not add further
simulations or classify the same records again to imply observational confirmation.
