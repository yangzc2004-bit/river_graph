DOC SPATIOTEMPORAL RECONSTRUCTION: CONSOLIDATION DECISION

The project is consolidated around reconstructing sparse river DOC by combining
environmental context, temporal memory and monitored source information. Temporal
and spatial missingness are parallel main experiments. The evaluated current-
source procedure is retained. This consolidation starts no training, changes no
model interface, and selects no new fitted model.

The main paper compares strong environmental trees, the previous complete
procedure and the current complete procedure. Temporal states are fitted for
their own observation roles, rather than importing the spatial deployment states.
Time results contain validation-selected concentration/fusion correction; that
correction is shown separately from native prediction. Spatial sources represent
ecological and hydrological similarity. The selected neural model uses no river
edge messages, and attention weights are not transport coefficients.

The strongest complete-method result is temporal reconstruction: MAE 0.785 and
0.797 mg/L, reducing error against the strong trees by 12.51% and 14.88%. Native
prediction nearly ties trees, so the fusion contribution is substantial. Regional
new-site MAE is 2.249 mg/L, reducing error by 4.11% against trees and 1.48% against
the previous complete method. Matched actual source values reduce regional error
by 0.97% against equally available seasonal values. The external fixed release
has MAE 0.911 mg/L; strong trees retain a lower 0.841 mg/L primary error. Five
designated local measurements reduce fixed-query error by 19.35%. These results
answer different task-weighted questions and are never combined into one score.

The scientific contributions are an environmental-temporal-source reconstruction
framework, observation-aware residual/source learning aligned with missing local
chemistry, and parallel temporal/geographical/external evaluation that resolves
information contributions. There is no new claim of universal graph superiority,
causal river-shape effects, active sampling or cross-analyte transfer.

Five core figures show tasks/architecture, temporal reconstruction, geographical
reconstruction, matched source information and external/support performance.
Environmental strata, high DOC, uncertainty/width and detailed controls are
supplementary. The river-morphology field studies and unsuccessful architectural
branches are closed as separate research archives. Their data and results remain
available, but they do not drive the current manuscript narrative.

The next research priorities, after completing this manuscript, are concentration-
regime transfer, higher-frequency high-DOC dynamics, additional basin replication
and river relationships supported by synchronized observations. This document
does not authorize a new training run. The current deliverable is a coherent
method paper and its reproducible evidence, not another architecture iteration.
