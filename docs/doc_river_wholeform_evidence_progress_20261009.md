# River whole-form research: evidence status and independent replication

The evidence consolidation and public-field-data audit are complete in
`experiments/phase4_transfer/doc_river_wholeform_evidence_v1/`.

The scientific status is **observed structural information, unresolved coarse
form ranking**. Branch organization adds 5.59% predictive MAE reduction above the
environment/area station-median diagnostic; all morphology adds 9.24%. Neither is
a released monthly-model gain or a causal effect. The direct 22-pair comparison
does not establish a broad-versus-elongated DOC ranking. Controlled real-path
routing establishes a conditional mechanism, not a field classification effect.

A new NEON-authored public archive supplies 27 river/stream candidates and 4,800
primary-location laboratory-DOC sampling occasions after combining replicates.
It enables preparation of an independent field concentration/variability test;
it does not resolve hourly pulse transmission. Exact chemistry outlets, complete
whole-network geometry, network independence and original source-quality context
still need verification. Site-center coordinates must not be used as validated
chemistry outlets; relocated observations remain separate.

The next bounded comparison is actual whole-network morphology versus DOC level
and variation on this independent field population, retaining environmental,
size and sampling-calendar context. The objective is repeatable added structural
information, not a compulsory universal class ordering. Report an inconclusive
or unsupported result and close this comparison if the available field design
cannot identify it. More simulations on the same records do not fill that gap.

No reconstruction training or automation was restarted. All earlier experiments
remain intact. Reproduction commands and the specific stopping decision are in
the experiment's English `research_decision.md`.
